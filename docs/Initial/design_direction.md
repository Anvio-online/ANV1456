## Our goal:

Premium technology company + modern agency + approachable enough for SMBs.

## Design personality

I'd define the brand as:

Modern
Premium
Technical
Confident
Minimal
Human

The website should communicate sophistication through spacing, typography, layout and motion, not through visual noise.

## Visual personality - Build. Automate. Grow.

These three should have their own visual identity without becoming three completely different designs.

BUILD

Visual language:

architecture
browser windows
website previews
product interfaces
grids
AUTOMATE

Visual language:

workflows
connected nodes
AI interactions
process diagrams
agent orchestration
GROW

Visual language:

analytics
search
dashboards
upward movement
performance metrics

So the visuals themselves communicate what the category means.


## Motion

This is where we can make the website feel expensive.

But motion should explain something, not just exist.

For example, your process section could animate:

DISCOVER
    ↓
PLAN
    ↓
BUILD
    ↓
LAUNCH
    ↓
OPTIMIZE

The Build section could show a website being assembled.

The Automate section could show:

Customer
    ↓
AI Agent
    ↓
CRM
    ↓
WhatsApp
    ↓
Team

The Grow section could show a metric/dashboard evolving.

Subtle scroll-triggered animation.

Nothing excessive.

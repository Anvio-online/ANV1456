One page I would also like to add 
Industries

Most agencies ignore this.

Imagine a visitor runs an accounting firm.

If they see:

AI Solutions

they might think,

"Is this really for my business?"

But if they see:

AI for Healthcare
AI for Ecommerce
AI for Real Estate
AI for Accounting
AI for Education

they immediately think,

"These people understand businesses like mine."

Eventually, each industry page can become a valuable SEO page.

Products (Very Important)

This page is not just for your future SaaS.

It also demonstrates your engineering capability.

Even if today you only have one or two tools, that's fine.

Over time it grows into your product portfolio.

One thing I would change from your original idea

You mentioned having AI Solutions as a landing page.

I would rename it to Automate.

Here's why:

"AI Solutions" describes what you use.

"Automate" describes what the client gets.

It's simpler and stronger.

For SEO, the page title can still be:

AI Automation Services

The URL can even be:

/services/automate

and internally target keywords like AI automation, AI solutions, AI agents, and business process automation.

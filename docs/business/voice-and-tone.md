# Voice and Tone

**Status:** v1 · Applies to every word Anvio publishes — site, guide, cold email, LinkedIn post, proposal.

---

## 1. The voice in one line

**A senior engineer explaining a business problem to a smart non-technical owner, without condescending and without showing off.**

---

## 2. Rules

**Concrete over abstract.** "Your team re-types 200 invoices a month" beats "operational inefficiency."

**Specific numbers, or no number.** "40+ hours a month" is a claim we can stand behind. "Save up to 90%!" is a claim nobody believes.

**Lead with the outcome, follow with the mechanism.** The stack is credibility shown _after_ trust, never the opener.

**Name the tradeoff.** Every recommendation has a cost. Saying it is the single cheapest trust-builder available and the thing that most distinguishes us from agencies that only sell upside.

**Short sentences. Ordinary words.** "Use" not "utilise." "Buy" not "procure."

**Second person.** "Your catalogue," not "the client's catalogue."

**Say what we won't do.** Refusals read as confidence — and they're already published in the commercial policies.

---

## 3. Banned

| Never                                                                                                                    | Because                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Cutting-edge", "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "synergy", "leverage" (as a verb), "unlock", "supercharge" | Every agency says them, so they carry no information                                         |
| "We're passionate about…"                                                                                                | Nobody buys passion                                                                          |
| Em-dash-heavy breathless copy, or exclamation marks in body copy                                                         | Reads as a newsletter, not an engineering company                                            |
| Emoji in site copy, proposals, or cold email                                                                             | LinkedIn/X may use them sparingly if the platform norm genuinely calls for it                |
| Fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake social proof                                                                           | Illegal in some markets, and fatal to a company selling trust                                |
| **Any client name, logo, metric, or testimonial we don't have**                                                          | See [current-situation.md](current-situation.md) — this is the one that gets checked hardest |
| "AI-powered" as a standalone benefit                                                                                     | It's a mechanism. What does it _do_ for them?                                                |

## 4. Per-channel calibration

Same voice, different length and opening.

| Channel        | Length                          | Opening move                                      | Ends with                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Site copy**  | Tight                           | The outcome                                       | The next page or the form                    |
| **Guides**     | 1,200–2,000 words               | The question they typed                           | An honest "when this doesn't apply"          |
| **Cold email** | Under 120 words                 | A specific observation about _their_ business     | One low-friction ask, never "hop on a call?" |
| **LinkedIn**   | 120–250 words                   | A concrete situation, no hook-baiting             | A real question, or nothing                  |
| **X/Twitter**  | 1 thought, or a 5–7 post thread | The claim, immediately                            | No CTA                                       |
| **Proposal**   | As long as the problem needs    | Their problem restated better than they stated it | Scope, price, and what's excluded            |
| **Newsletter** | 300–600 words                   | One idea                                          | One link                                     |

---

## 5. The test

Before publishing anything, two questions:

1. **Could a competitor put their logo on this unchanged?** If yes, it says nothing — rewrite.
2. **Does this move toward or away from _"these people aren't freelancers, they look like a product engineering company"_?** ([positioning.md](positioning.md) §3.)
